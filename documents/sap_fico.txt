
SAP FICO is a financial accounting and controlling module in SAP ERP.

SAP FI stands for Financial Accounting. It supports general ledger
accounting, accounts payable, accounts receivable, and asset accounting.

SAP CO stands for Controlling. It supports cost centers, internal
orders, cost analysis, and management accounting.

A company code is an organizational unit used to represent an
independent accounting entity in SAP.

A chart of accounts is a structured list of general ledger accounts.
A fiscal year variant defines the accounting periods for a fiscal year.
